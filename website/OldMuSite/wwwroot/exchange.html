Item Exchange

Give in 3 excellent items you don't need – get the excellent item you want.

← Back to the guide

How it works

  1. Put 3 excellent items with a drop level of 44 or more into your inventory (not equipped). Which items count?
  2. Choose an item below and type its command, e.g. /exchange Dragon Armor. The game shows which items would be given in – the cheapest first.
  3. Confirm with yes at the end: /exchange Dragon Armor yes.

Keep the excellent items you want to keep in the warehouse while you exchange.

What you get

  • Item level+0
  • One excellent optionalways
  • Two excellent options25%
  • Three excellent options5%
  • Luck50%
  • Additional optionrandom, up to +12

Weapons get weapon options (e.g. excellent damage rate, +2% damage, attack speed, life or mana after a kill), armor and shields get defense options (e.g. max HP +4%, damage decrease, reflect, zen after a kill).
