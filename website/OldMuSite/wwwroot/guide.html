Getting started

  1. Create an account on the registration page – a login and a password, no e-mail.
  2. Download the game, unpack it anywhere and start OldMuLauncher.exe.
  3. Press Start Game, log in and create your character: Dark Knight, Dark Wizard, Fairy Elf or Magic Gladiator. The Magic Gladiator can be created right away.
  4. You start in Lorencia (Elves in Noria). Hunt, level up, and switch on the MU Helper to hunt for you.

Type the commands of this guide into the chat (Enter, then the command). /list shows all commands in the game, /help /reset explains one of them.

Party: every party member near the kill gets the full experience, as much as when hunting alone – the experience isn't split.

Stats

Every level gives you points for strength, agility, vitality and energy. Add them in the character window (C) or faster with a command:

/addstr 100Adds 100 points to strength.
/addagi 100Agility.
/addvit 100Vitality.
/addene 100Energy.

Resets

When your character is strong enough, reset it to grow even stronger:

  • Type /reset from level 350. Every reset needs 10 levels more, up to level 400.
  • A reset is free. Your character goes back to level 10, its stats are reset, and you get 500 points for every reset you have (5 resets = 2,500 points). You are moved to town and logged out.
  • At most 55 resets. The more resets you have, the less experience you get.
  • /resetinfo shows what your next reset needs and gives.

Events

All events are free – no tickets. Times are Georgian time (Tbilisi, UTC+4). The entrance is open for 5 minutes; you are told in the game 5 and 2 minutes before.

You can enter Devil Square and Blood Castle once per day each. You play in the room of your level, against players of your level, and the experience is doubled there. The best players get Silver Medals.

Silver medals

Silver Medals are the reward of the events and the bosses. They go into your bag; when it's full, they are kept for you and paid out at Thompson.

  • Devil Square: 1st place 4 medals, 2nd and 3rd place 2.
  • Blood Castle: the winner 3, the rest of the winning party 1.
  • Kill at least 50 monsters in one Devil Square or Blood Castle game and stay until it ends: 1 extra Silver Medal, on top of the others.
  • Team vs Team: the best player 3, the winning team 1.
  • Bosses: 1 Silver Medal, the Dark Phoenix and Hell Maine 6 (see Bosses).

Thompson the Merchant stands next to the fountain of Lorencia and trades medals for boxes:

Box of Kundun +35 Silver Medals
Box of Kundun +410 Silver Medals

PvP

  • The PvP spots are areas where killing players gives prestige and doesn't make you a killer. /zones lists them.
  • Winning a duel gives prestige too.
  • /ranking shows the ranking of this week, /ranking all of all time. It's also on the ranking page.
  • A killer (PK) outside the PvP spots can clear the status with /pkclear: 10,000,000 zen per kill.

Castle Siege

Every week the guilds fight for the castle in the Valley of Loren (times are Tbilisi time):

  • Monday: the guild masters register their guild at the guardsman at the bridge of the Valley of Loren. The guild master needs level 200 and the guild 20 members.
  • Wednesday: the registered guilds hand in their Signs of Lord (Rena +3, dropped by monsters from level 50). Up to 3 guilds with the most signs and members attack.
  • Saturday 20:00 – 22:00: the battle. The guild whose master last takes the crown owns the castle when the battle ends and collects its taxes.

Every guild member who fought at least 30 minutes gets 5 Boxes of Kundun +4, also when offline at the end. Pamela and Angela in the Valley of Loren sell potions to the attackers and the defenders. The Chaos Goblin mixes the Life Stone and the siege potions.

Item exchange (list)

Turn excellent items you don't need into the excellent item you want. With /exchange you give in 3 excellent items with a drop level of 44 or more and get one excellent item of your choice.

  1. Put the excellent items to give in into your inventory (not equipped). Keep the ones you want to keep in the warehouse – the cheapest items are taken first.
  2. Type /exchange to see the items you can get.
  3. Type /exchange Dragon Armor. The game shows which of your items would be given in.
  4. Confirm with /exchange Dragon Armor yes. The new item is in your inventory.

The new item is +0 with at least one excellent option. The other options are random. See the list of items and what counts →

Offline leveling

Your character can keep hunting while the game is closed:

  1. Go to your hunting spot and start the MU Helper.
  2. Type /offlevel. The game closes and your character keeps hunting with your MU Helper settings.
  3. Log in again to stop it.

It doesn't work in events, and your character has to be alive. The offline character counts as one of the two accounts of your IP.

Chat & guilds

/world textWrites to all players of all servers. The normal chat only reaches the players near you.
/post textSends your text as a blue message to all players.
/war guildAsks the master of another guild for a guild war.
/battlesoccer guildAsks another guild for a battle soccer match.

Rules

  • At most 2 accounts per IP can be in the game at the same time (offline leveling counts).
  • Only one account per IP may enter each event.
  • Items can be upgraded up to +11.
  • No cheats, bots or bug abuse. Respect the other players.
